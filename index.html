AI Lab

AI Lab is a set of seven tools I designed and built myself, all running inside this page. I wanted a portfolio that does something instead of only describing it, so every tool here is a working project you can use right now.

The chat, reply, translation, story and photo description tools are powered by Claude through the page, so my work went into the interface, the prompts and the flow. The HD / 4K enhancer and the video frame grabber are my own code and run completely on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded anywhere.

Every tool is built mobile first: big tap targets, one action at a time, and results you can save with a single tap.

Seven tools in one place. Pick a tab.

I built this assistant to answer visitors automatically, at any time of day. It replies in your language, keeps a short memory of the conversation and stays in character for this site. Type a question and it responds within seconds.

Auto Reply is a writing assistant I made for busy inboxes. Paste any message you received, pick a tone, and get a ready-to-send reply in the same language. It works well for customer messages, client emails and everyday chats.

A fast translator I built for the languages I use most. Paste your text, choose a target language and get a clean translation with nothing extra added, ready to copy straight into a chat.

This one is for fun and fits the theme of the site. Give it a topic and it writes a short horror story from scratch. Press Read aloud and your phone narrates it in a low, slow voice. Best with headphones at night.

I built this tool to save time on captions. Choose a photo from your gallery and it describes what is in the picture, then suggests three short captions you can post.

My on-device photo enhancer. Choose a photo from your gallery and buttons for HD, 2K and 4K appear. Tap one and the image is enlarged in several steps, sharpened and turned into a download button. It does not invent new detail, so a very blurry photo stays soft, but ordinary photos come out noticeably cleaner at the larger size.

Choose a photo. HD, 2K and 4K buttons will appear.
Upscaling and sharpening run on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

A small studio for video stills. Choose a video from your gallery, pause on the exact moment you want, and save that frame at full resolution or enlarged to 4K. Handy for thumbnails, covers and screenshots.

Pause on any moment, then grab the frame.
